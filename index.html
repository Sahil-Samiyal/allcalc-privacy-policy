AllCalc Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

AllCalc ("we", "us", or "our") is a calculator application developed by Sahil Samiyal. This Privacy Policy explains how AllCalc handles information when you use the application.

1. Information Stored by AllCalc

AllCalc does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal profile information to use the application.

Calculator History

AllCalc can save your calculator history on your device. This may include:

  • Calculator expressions you enter
  • Calculation results
  • The time a calculation was saved

This information is used to provide the calculation-history feature. Calculator history is stored locally by the application and is not intentionally transmitted by AllCalc to our own servers.

You can delete individual history entries or clear your calculation history using the controls provided by the application.

Application Settings

AllCalc may store application preferences locally on your device. These preferences are used to provide the requested application functionality.

2. Currency Exchange Rates

The currency conversion feature can retrieve exchange-rate information from the Frankfurter API.

The application uses these rates to provide currency-conversion calculations. Retrieved exchange-rate information may be cached locally on your device.

AllCalc does not intentionally send your calculator history, name, email address, phone number, or other personal profile information to the currency-rate service.

3. Advertising

AllCalc uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising services may process certain information from your device and your interaction with advertisements or the application for purposes such as advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation.

Depending on applicable settings, consent, and Google's policies, this may include information such as advertising or device identifiers, app interaction information, and diagnostic information.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

AllCalc uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy and consent requirements for advertising.

Where required, users may be presented with a privacy or consent message before advertisements are requested.

Where applicable, AllCalc provides a privacy-options mechanism that allows users to revisit their advertising privacy choices.

For more information, see Google's UMP documentation .

5. Internet and Network Access

AllCalc requires network access for certain features, including:

  • Retrieving currency exchange rates
  • Loading advertisements
  • Services required by the advertising SDK

Ordinary calculator calculations can be performed without a network connection.

6. Data Sharing

AllCalc does not operate its own user account system and does not sell your personal information.

However, third-party services integrated into the application, particularly Google Mobile Ads, may independently collect or process information as described in their respective privacy policies and documentation.

The currency-rate service receives requests necessary to provide exchange-rate information.

7. Data Security

AllCalc stores calculator history and application preferences locally on your device.

Network communications used by the application and its third-party services may use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS where supported by those services.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Calculator history and application preferences remain on your device according to the application's functionality and your device's storage and backup settings.

You can delete calculator history using the controls provided in AllCalc.

Uninstalling AllCalc generally removes the application's local data from the device, subject to Android's behavior and applicable device backup or restoration features.

Information processed independently by third-party services, such as Google, is subject to those services' own retention and deletion policies.

9. Children's Privacy

AllCalc is a general-purpose calculator application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information through the application.

Advertising and privacy handling may be subject to additional requirements depending on the user's age, location, and applicable regulations.

10. Third-Party Services

AllCalc uses third-party services including:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
    https://developers.google.com/admob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
    https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy
  • Frankfurter API
    https://www.frankfurter.dev/

These third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when AllCalc's functionality, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
